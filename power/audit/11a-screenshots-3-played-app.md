## Summary

1. Contrast: stat-band numbers render dark on teal or dark on dark on Command, Team, Benefits, Financials and Quarter close; all Settings inputs and every dropdown placeholder are pale grey and unreadable.
2. Dead vertical space: fixed-height cards leave 300 to 600px empty below short lists on Team Squads, Quarter close History and Archive, Timeline Sprints, Benefits and Settings.
3. Clipping and truncation: Timeline squad summaries and cell titles, the last gallery row on Work and Quarter close, Team's Free column, and band subtitles all cut off.
4. Panels: all four side panels show their validation error before any input, hundreds of pixels from the field, with the primary button disabled and dropdowns showing no placeholder.
5. Badges and data: pills are oversized, vertically off-centre and colour-only; counts disagree across screens (36 vs 28 people, 18 vs 20 vs 7 items, 45.3 vs 46.3 MW reserved) and "test user" is in the seed data.
